# Pulse Dodge — Neon Arena

Fast, lightweight 60fps arcade dodge game built with pure HTML5 Canvas2D, CSS3, ES Modules, and Web Audio synthesis.

## Controls

- **Move**: Mouse, Touch drag, or `WASD` / `Arrow Keys`
- **Pulse Blast** (when charge bar reaches 100%): `Space` or Double-Tap
- **Pause**: `P` / `Esc` or Pause button

## Mechanics

- **Energy Cores**: Collect cyan orbs to score points, raise your combo multiplier (`x1`–`x12`), and charge Pulse Blast.
- **Graze Bonus**: Skim close to active drones for extra points and Blast charge.
- **Telegraphed Spawns**: Drones show a warning ring before becoming active.
