garden-01 | Argue: Ants or bees are bugs with antennae that look like extra legs | Holds: True insects strictly have 6 legs while spiders (arachnids) have 8 legs.
garden-02 | Argue: Mushrooms grow from dirt in the ground like plants | Holds: Fungi lack chlorophyll and cannot perform photosynthesis like true green plants.
garden-03 | Argue: Platypuses are mammals that lay eggs so bears might too | Holds: Bears and foxes are placental mammals giving live birth; turtles and eagles lay eggs.
garden-04 | Argue: Bats are furry mammals and not real flying birds | Holds: Bats have powered wing flight; snails only crawl on ground.
garden-05 | Argue: Chrysalis looks like a cocoon or stone | Holds: Butterflies strictly form a hanging chrysalis (pupa) from a twig before emerging as adult butterflies.
garden-06 | Argue: Leaves appear before stems or roots sprout downwards | Holds: Germination universally begins with seed sprouting root/shoot before growing into sunflower.
sky-01 | Argue: Mercury is closest to the Sun so it must be hottest | Holds: Venus's dense greenhouse atmosphere traps heat, making it hotter than Mercury.
sky-02 | Argue: Moon is bright in sky so it could be our closest star | Holds: Moon reflects light and planets lack fusion; Sun is Earth's only daytime star.
sky-03 | Argue: The Moon shines brightly in the night sky | Holds: Moon emits no light of its own and purely reflects sunlight.
sky-04 | Argue: Only Saturn has rings visible in pictures | Holds: Jupiter, Uranus, and Neptune all possess planetary ring systems.
sky-05 | Argue: Earth gets hot in summer so it might be closer than Venus | Holds: Planetary orbital distances from Sun strictly follow Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars.
sky-06 | Argue: The Sun is a star so maybe other stars are closer than the Sun | Holds: Moon is 239,000 miles, Sun is our closest star at 93 million miles, and all other stars are light-years farther.
body-01 | Argue: Big adults have bigger bodies so they have more bones | Holds: Newborn babies start with ~300 bones that fuse into 206 adult bones.
body-02 | Argue: Skin is outer wrapping rather than an organ | Holds: Skin is anatomically the largest organ of the human body.
body-03 | Argue: Big bones hold up body weight so bone is hardest | Holds: Tooth enamel is 96% mineral crystal, physically harder than bone or keratin nails.
body-04 | Argue: Eyes help you enjoy taste of food | Holds: Chemoreceptors for taste and smell are strictly in tongue taste buds and nose.
body-05 | Argue: Some babies are born with natal teeth | Holds: Overwhelmingly babies are born toothless (0), toddlers develop 20 primary teeth, and adults have up to 32 permanent teeth.
body-06 | Argue: Swallowing happens while chewing food | Holds: Mechanical chewing with teeth strictly precedes swallowing down throat into stomach.
