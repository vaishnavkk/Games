# Robot Factory Defense — Version 0.2 Backlog & Candidate Features

This document holds ideas, enhancements, and expansions deferred from v0.1 to keep scope frozen and verified.

## 1. Gameplay & Mechanics
- **Endless Survival Mode**: Post-wave 15 infinite scaling waves with leaderboards.
- **Multiple Factory Maps**: Forking conveyor belts, multi-entrance layouts, conveyor switches.
- **Dynamic Weather / Factory Hazards**: Steam vents that temporarily boost/slow towers or robots.
- **Boss Special Abilities**: EMP blasts disabling nearby towers, shield drones, armor repairs.

## 2. Audio & Atmosphere
- **Industrial Sound Effects**: Hydraulic thuds, plasma hums, laser pulses, rocket explosions (synthesized Web Audio or CC0 samples).
- **Procedural Synth BGM**: Dynamic techno-industrial background tracks adapting to wave intensity.

## 3. Visuals & Polish
- **Advanced Shaders / Canvas Filters**: Bloom on arc lightning, heat distortion on rocket trails.
- **Damage Numbers & Flying Text**: Optional floating combat text with accessibility toggle.
- **Achievements & Badges**: Clean Factory (no core damage), Circuit Mogul (100+ circuits), Scrap Hoarder.
