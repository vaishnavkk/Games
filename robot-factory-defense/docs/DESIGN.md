# Robot Factory Defense — Game Design Document (v0.1)

## 1. Grid & Map
- **Layout**: 16 columns × 9 rows grid (48px logical cell size).
- **Conveyor Path**: Fixed winding path with 4 turns (0,2)->(12,2)->(12,5)->(3,5)->(3,7)->(15,7). Path length: 39 cells. No runtime pathfinding.
- **Buildable Cells**: 28 dedicated assembly pads bordering the conveyor track.
- **Core Base**: Located at grid exit (15, 7) with 20 starting HP.

## 2. Economy & Currency
- **Starting Capital**: 150 Scrap, 0 Circuits.
- **Scrap**: Earned by destroying rogue automata; spent on building and upgrading towers.
- **Circuits**: Generated by Fabricators (1 Circuit / 10s, max 4 placed on buildable cells); spent in Workshop.
- **Sell Refund**: floor(70% of the Scrap actually paid for that tower including its upgrades).

## 3. Defensive Towers & Combat Rules
All combat towers feature 3 progression levels with increasing costs and capabilities:
1. **Pulse Turret**: Rapid single-target kinetic fire (Level 1: 60 Scrap, 12 DMG, 1.5/s, 2.5 RNG; upgrades: 50/80).
2. **Coolant Sprayer**: Cryogenic mist dealing light damage and 50% speed slow for 2.0s without stacking (Level 1: 80 Scrap, 4 DMG, 1.0/s, 2.2 RNG; upgrades: 60/90).
3. **Arc Coil**: High-voltage lightning chaining up to 3 targets within 1.5 cells, completely ignoring armor (Unlocked at Workshop for 20 Circuits; Level 1: 100 Scrap, 8 DMG, 1.0/s, 2.4 RNG; upgrades: 80/120).
4. **Rocket Rack**: Devastating long-range artillery with 1.2-cell area splash (Unlocked at Workshop for 40 Circuits; Level 1: 120 Scrap, 35 DMG, 0.4/s, 3.5 RNG; upgrades: 95/140).
5. **Fabricator**: Passive industrial generator producing 1 Circuit / 10s (Cost: 75 Scrap, max 4 total).
- **Damage Rule**: Damage dealt = max(1, damage - armour). Arc Coil ignores armour.

## 4. Adversaries & Waves (15 Waves Total)
- **Glitchbot**: 30 HP, 2.2 speed, 0 armour, 6 scrap, 1 core leak damage. Erratic runner.
- **Rust Walker**: 60 HP, 1.4 speed, 2 armour, 8 scrap, 1 core leak damage. Standard workhorse.
- **Spark Drone**: 50 HP, 2.0 speed, 0 armour, 10 scrap, 1 core leak damage. Fast agile flyer.
- **Crusher Tank**: 180 HP, 0.9 speed, 6 flat armour, 20 scrap, 3 core leak damage. Heavy siege automaton.
- **Iron Sentry (Wave 10 Mini-Boss)**: 1200 HP, 0.7 speed, 8 armour, 80 scrap, 5 core leak damage.
- **Mega Mixer (Wave 15 Boss)**: 3500 HP, 0.5 speed, 10 armour, 200 scrap, 10 core leak damage.
- **Wave Pacing**: Wave spawn gap: 5s. Target wave duration: 40-75s sim time at x1 for competent bot (~12-20 min game).

## 5. Workshop & Research
Six one-time global upgrades purchased exclusively with Circuits:
- **Overclock** (30 Circuits): +15% turret fire rate.
- **Hardened Rounds** (35 Circuits): +15% turret damage.
- **Long Barrels** (25 Circuits): +10% turret range.
- **Scrap Magnet** (20 Circuits): +20% Scrap yield from kills.
- **Repair Bay** (20 Circuits): +5 Core HP immediately.
- **Fast Assembly** (30 Circuits): -10% turret build/upgrade Scrap cost (affects only future purchases).
- **Tower Unlocks**: Arc Coil (20 Circuits), Rocket Rack (40 Circuits). Total Workshop: 220 Circuits (160 upgrades + 60 unlocks).

## 6. Flow, UI & Controls
- **Screens**: Title -> Factory (HUD: Core HP, Scrap, Circuits, Wave n/15, Start Wave, Speed x1/x2, Pause, Workshop modal) -> Win / Defeat summary -> Retry.
- **Input**: Strictly tap/click only. Tap cell -> build bar; tap tower -> inspect, upgrade, sell. Targets >= 48px.
- **First-Run Tips (<= 12 words each)**:
  1. "Tap a green cell, then select a turret to build defense."
  2. "Build Fabricators early to generate Circuits for powerful Workshop upgrades."
  3. "Armored enemies resist basic turrets; use Arc Coils and Rockets."
- **Persistence**: `localStorage` keys prefixed with `rfd:` (`rfd:best_wave`, `rfd:wins`), wrapped in try/catch.
- **Accessibility & Rendering**: Procedural Canvas2D in code; shapes plus colors for color-blind safety; CSS `prefers-reduced-motion` respected; strictly zero audio.
